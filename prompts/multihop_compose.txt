Combine the following two single-hop questions into one natural multi-hop question.

## Hop 1 ({hop1_modality}, page {hop1_page}):
Question: {hop1_question}
Answer: {hop1_answer}

## Hop 2 ({hop2_modality}, page {hop2_page}):
Question: {hop2_question}
Answer: {hop2_answer}

## Bridge entity: "{bridge_entity}"
Hop 1's answer leads to Hop 2: the answer "{hop1_answer}" is the entity "{bridge_entity}" mentioned in Hop 2's question.

## Your task:
Create a single natural multi-hop question by turning Hop 1 into a descriptive clause that identifies the bridge entity WITHOUT naming it, and Hop 2 into the main question.

**Structure**: Use Hop 1 as a subordinate/relative clause, and Hop 2 as the main question body:
- "For the [Hop1 indirect description], [Hop2 question about its attribute]?"
- "What is the [attribute] of the [Hop1 indirect description]?"
- "According to the report, the [Hop1 indirect description] had what [attribute]?"

**Bad example** (two questions joined by "and"):
"Which group had the highest rate in 2015, and what was their income?"

**Good example** (Hop1 becomes a descriptive clause):
"For the group with the highest rate in 2015, what was their median household income?"

## Rules:
1. Do NOT mention "{bridge_entity}" by name
2. Hop 1 must become a descriptive clause, NOT a separate question
3. The result must read as ONE single question, not two questions joined by "and"
4. Keep it concise and natural
5. Do NOT reference any page numbers, layout IDs, figure numbers, or table numbers (e.g., "page 19", "Figure 3", "Table 2"). The question must be self-contained without document structure references

Return JSON only:
{{
  "composed_question": "<the multi-hop question>",
  "final_answer": "<Hop 2's answer>",
  "indirect_description": "<the phrase you used to replace the bridge entity>"
}}
