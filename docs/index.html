Abstract

TopoGraphRAG-Bench evaluates multimodal GraphRAG on layout-grounded evidence reasoning across text, figures, and tables. Questions are organized into three reasoning topologies: single-hop retrieval, bridge-chain reasoning, and multi-source synthesis.
